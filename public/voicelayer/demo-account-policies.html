My account FAQ

Billing and records

How do I pay my bill?

You can pay online from the billing tab, by phone with the front desk, or in person. We accept major credit cards and bank transfers. Payment plans are available for balances over $200.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans. Bring your insurance card to your first visit so the front desk can verify your coverage before you are seen.

What is your refund policy?

Refunds for overpayments are issued to the original payment method within ten business days. Late cancellation fees are not refundable except in documented emergencies.
