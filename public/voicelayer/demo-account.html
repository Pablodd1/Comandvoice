Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?

The front desk is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, and Saturday from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

How do I reschedule an appointment?

You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your visit by calling the front desk or using the appointments page. Changes made inside 24 hours may be charged a late fee. Please bring your member ID to every visit.

Is there parking at the clinic?

Yes. Free parking is available behind the building, with accessible spaces next to the side entrance. Street parking is limited.

How can I get a copy of my records?

Request your records from the account page or at the front desk. Copies are usually ready within five business days. We need a signed release form before sending records to another provider.

Policies

Cancellation policy

Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment to avoid a late cancellation fee of $25. Emergencies are reviewed case by case. Repeated no-shows may affect your ability to book online.

Privacy policy

We share your information only with your care team and as required by law. You can ask us for a list of who has accessed your record. This assistant does not store health information.
